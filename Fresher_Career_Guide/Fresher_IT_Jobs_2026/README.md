# Fresher IT Jobs Application Guide

A quick reference PDF for freshers exploring graduate and entry-level roles at TCS, Wipro, Accenture, Infosys, and Cognizant in India.

## Included in the PDF

- Official company career and application links
- Qualification and eligibility checkpoints
- Skills to prepare for each company’s fresher pathways
- Reminders for checking openings and applying safely
- Five practical Resume Tips for freshers


## Important

Openings, eligible batches, qualifications, and conditions vary by role and may change. Check each company’s official live job listing before applying. TCS’s 2026 NQT page was marked closed when this guide was prepared.
