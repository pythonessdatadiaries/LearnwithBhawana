# AI & Tech Hackathon Opportunities — October 2026

A beginner-friendly list of AI and technology hackathons, with deadlines, eligibility notes, project requirements, and official links.

Hackathons can help you practise building real projects, work with a team, and create something to discuss in your portfolio or interviews. They do not guarantee a job, prize, or recruiter attention.

## Important

- Deadlines, eligibility, prizes, and event rules can change.
- Dates and details in this list were checked on October 8, 2026.
- Always confirm the latest information on the official event page before applying.
- Prize pools are not guaranteed earnings.
- Participation does not guarantee a job.
- For any submission, follow the organizer’s rules on originality, licensing, team membership, and required deliverables.

## Beginner tips

1. Pick a problem you can explain in one or two sentences.
2. Choose a small feature and aim to make it work.
3. Keep your code and project notes organized.
4. Add setup and run instructions to your README.
5. Be clear about your own contribution and what you learned.
