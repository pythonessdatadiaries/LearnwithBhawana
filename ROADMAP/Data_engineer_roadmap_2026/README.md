# Data Engineering Roadmap 2026

A practical roadmap for learning data engineering, with separate paths for freshers and experienced professionals.

The guide lays out what to learn, when to learn it, and what small projects to build along the way. 
It includes free YouTube and learning resources, plus selected Udemy, Coursera, and DataCamp courses with ratings and fee notes.


# How to use it

1. Pick the fresher or experienced path that fits you.
2. Follow the phases in order, adjusting the pace to your schedule.
3. Build the suggested projects and save them in a portfolio.
4. Check course fees and current job requirements before making decisions; prices and role expectations can change.

This roadmap is a learning guide, not a promise of interviews or a job. Focus on practicing the skills and showing what you can build.
